# Product

## Platform

web

## Users
Private landlords (one to a handful of properties), letting agents, HMO owners, and secondarily homeowners and small shop/office owners, within 25 miles of Whitechapel (E1), East London. They arrive because a certificate is due, a tenancy is starting, a council HMO licence asks for something, or an EICR came back with codes. The job: get the right certificates done in one visit at a known price, without having to be at the property, and stay legally compliant.

## Product Purpose
A local compliance trade business: gas safety certificates (CP12), EICRs, fire alarm and emergency lighting tests, fire risk assessments, EPCs, PAT testing, boiler service, and the remedial work when something fails. Success is a landlord getting an exact price on the site and booking (or calling/WhatsApping) without needing to ask anything first.

## Positioning
One qualified engineer, registered for both gas and electrical, does every check in one visit and is the person you speak to. Not a booking platform or contractor network. Prices are fixed and visible before booking, with a bundle discount for two or more certificates.

## Operating Context
Landlords usually book remotely and arrange tenant access by phone. They receive certificates by email and must give tenants copies within set deadlines (28 days for EICR). Renewal cycles: gas yearly, EICR up to five years, EPC ten years. EICR reports carry C1/C2/C3/FI codes that drive remedial work.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Static site generated by `build.py` (Python, no dependencies) into `dist/`, deployed on Vercel. CSS and JS are inlined per page.
- Interactive pieces: price picker (pricing maths mirrored in `build.py` and `src/main.js`), EICR code checker, quote/booking/reminder forms posting JSON to `form_endpoint`.
- Business facts and prices live in `site.config.json`; many are still TODO and render highlighted until filled in.
- Sister brand Emergency Services 24/7 handles urgent electrical repairs.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Landlord Safety First (landlordsafetyfirst.uk). Shield-and-tick logo mark in `build.py` (`LOGO`) and `src/static/`.
- Voice: plain English, sentence case, the law explained simply, no hype. UK spelling, prices shown inc. VAT with ex-VAT beneath.
- Feel (confirmed by user, 2026-11-15): trusted professional; calm, precise, document-like. Redesign of the visual world approved; content, prices and functions stay.

## Evidence on Hand
- No engineer photo yet (`engineer_photo` empty); no reviews yet; registration numbers, phone and insurance are TODO. Future work must not invent reviews, ratings, registration numbers, customer counts or years trading.
- Real assets: logo, favicon, OG image in `src/static/`; a generated service-area map (SVG) in `build.py`.

## Product Principles
1. Price first: the visitor should see an exact price before being asked for anything.
2. Prove with the paperwork: certificates, codes, dates and deadlines are the evidence, not adjectives.
3. One person, one visit: every surface should make the single-engineer model obvious.
4. Plain law: explain obligations in plain English with dates and deadlines.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA. Keyboard-operable picker and checker, visible focus, reduced-motion respected, works on small phones (many landlords book from a phone).
