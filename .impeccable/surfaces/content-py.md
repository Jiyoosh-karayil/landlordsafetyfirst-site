# Home (/) and service landing pages

Scope: homepage plus the shared page shell (header, page-head, side price card, quote form, footer) that every service page inherits. Mode: Persuade. Traffic is mostly Google Ads, so the first viewport must convert on its own.

Audience and job: a landlord or letting agent who clicked an ad for "EICR / gas safety certificate East London"; wants an exact price and a slot, often on a phone.
Action: tick certificates, see the total, Book. Secondary: call or WhatsApp.
Proof on hand: fixed prices, bundle maths, one engineer for gas and electrical, 25-mile coverage map. No reviews or registration numbers yet; do not invent them.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the property's compliance register. The working price picker is the hero, set as a ruled register sheet, not a split hero with a decorative card, a trust-badge row and an icon-card grid.

OWN-WORLD: White register sheets on a cool grey-white ground, navy #0F2A44 as the binding (header band, summary foot, close), ink near-navy text, hairline grey rules carrying all structure, amber #F5A524 reserved for the single Book action and the coverage edging on the map. Source Serif 4 for headings and totals; Public Sans for everything else. No drop-shadow cards: depth is a sheet on a ground, rules inside the sheet.

STORY: In one viewport the visitor reads what this is (landlord safety certificates, East London), sees a real price for a 2-bed flat already filled in, and can book it. Scrolling, they see bundles, the full ledger of services with prices, the four-step visit, why a single engineer, the coverage plan, FAQ, close.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: left 5/12 serif H1 at about 4rem, two-line lede, credentials line, call and WhatsApp as quiet text buttons. Right 7/12 the register sheet: property type tabs, bedrooms, nine certificate entries as ruled rows with prices, foot band in navy with saving, total in serif, amber Book. Mobile: H1, one-line lede, then the sheet, Book visible within about 1.3 screens; sticky call/book bar stays.

FORM: Land Registry title register, my 7th grounded candidate, assigned by the roll. seed:c7ae60b2007b00ed. Raises: from the sewing-pattern envelope, selected entries draw solid and unselected stay dashed; from the cutting-bench rail, state is a mark not a hue; from the papercut, no shadows, depth only by sheet overlap. Signature interaction: ticking an entry writes it into the register (rule turns solid, price inks in, total recounts).
